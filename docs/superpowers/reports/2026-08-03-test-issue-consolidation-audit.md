# Test-Issue Consolidation Acceptance-Coverage Audit

## Snapshot and method

This audit maps every acceptance criterion in the bodies of issues #153 through
#168 to the tests on `origin/main` at
[`08ead258e291b21706b9197c666a81def0d850e2`](https://github.com/tokyogas-tech/hoimin/commit/08ead258e291b21706b9197c666a81def0d850e2).
All sixteen issues were open when queried on 2026-08-03. A criterion is
`covered` only when the current test crosses the boundary requested by the
issue. A related fix tested at a weaker boundary is listed but remains
`missing`. `platform remainder` is reserved for the explicit Windows/backend
exceptions allowed by the approved consolidation design.

The audit found 12 covered criteria, 46 missing criteria, and 2 platform
remainders. Test links are commit-pinned; PR links identify the merged fix that
introduced or is most directly related to the cited regression coverage.
