## Delivered contract and ownership

`DiskRunSummary.janitor` replaces `stale_roots_reclaimed`. It is required nullable: null means no startup observation; Some with zero counts can mean an observed empty scan. A field-level required deserializer rejects missing fields, separately from accepting explicit null. JSON Schema requires the field and admits null or the closed structured summary. The actual shared artifact reader and schema tests cover both cases independently.

The janitor finalizes one bounded ledger into `JanitorSummary`. Each inspected candidate has exactly one final outcome: reclaimed, already absent, ordinary preserved, or unresolved. Counts and reason maps are folded once from these outcomes; `inspected_roots` equals their sum. Positive ordinary holds are young, active lease, retain marker, coordinator busy, and Windows mutation barrier active. Inspection/integrity/claim/deletion failures and unfinished cleanup stay visible by default. A malformed heartbeat now explicitly produces unresolved/heartbeat_invalid rather than the old misleading preserved count; its filesystem remains untouched.

Transient `DeletionProgress` belongs to cleanup capabilities and records traversal observations and acknowledged deletion operations at the actual mutation boundaries. It survives errors through marker removal, single-entry traversal, slices, bounded loops, and post-deletion verification. Root acknowledgement is consumed into a final reclaimed outcome and is not retained as a contradictory final flag. An unlock or verification error remains a diagnostic even when the root was deleted. Absence never invents a deletion. A partial failure retains the number of acknowledged descendants/markers; success does not manufacture an item count from the number of roots.

Removed entries include files, symlinks, directories, control markers and root entries acknowledged by the OS. They exclude inspections, rename, chmod, attempted operations and NotFound. On Windows an accepted deletion can still be pending because handles remain open; neither removed entries nor reclaimed roots establish physical space release or deletion of a same-name replacement. Examined entries are traversal observations and may revisit names. No total remaining-root or freed-byte estimate is introduced.

Selection retains its existing two bounded name sets, cursor policy, 256-root batch, 100,000-child enumeration limit, five-second selection and thirty-second cleanup budgets. The scan exposes observed matching entries, selected roots, selected-but-uninspected roots, observed selection omissions, cursor deferral, selection-limit evidence, enumeration completion and typed stop reason. Interrupted enumeration has an unknown unobserved tail. Enumeration completion does not imply all candidates were inspected. Exactly 256 selected names does not imply an extra root exists.

The detail owner retains at most 256 records of at most 4096 UTF-8 bytes each. A late issue evicts an ordinary detail before losing issue evidence. Normal/issue capacity losses, string truncations and presentation suppression have separate counters. Counts do not depend on retained strings. The common `ReportHandler` boundary projects the owned RunFinished event before choosing JSON, JSONL or human output. Default suppresses ordinary holds; verbose exposes retained ordinary details within the same bounds. Failed/deferred current cleanup and measurement causes remain visible, including paths, in human output. Human fields use Task 2's escaping; JSON/JSONL preserve their original strings.

Startup facts no longer enter the execution cleanup row. Current-run `CleanupDisposition`, cleanup retry eligibility, exit policy, claim/deletion syscall ordering, fallback checks and deferred-only `round_removed` scheduling remain unchanged. Owner cleanup uses the same improved acknowledgement evidence but its disposition match is preserved.
